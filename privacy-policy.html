Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Life Touch is built around one rule: your data stays on your device. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what that means — what the app stores, what it never does, and what happens when you use features like data export or the AI prompt generator.

01The short version

Nothing you log in Life Touch ever leaves your phone. There is no server, no account, and no sign-in. The app works fully offline. It shows you your own raw data — timestamps, frequencies, simple patterns — and never interprets, diagnoses, or gives medical advice. If you want a professional opinion on what your data means, you take it to a doctor or an AI tool of your own choosing; Life Touch doesn't do that part for you.

The rest of this page spells that out in detail, section by section.

02Data we collect

Kabastro, the developer of Life Touch, does not collect, receive, or have access to any of the data you log in the app. We operate no backend server for user data, so there is nothing on our side to collect.

Everything you enter into the app — habit taps and their timestamps, custom habits you create, notes, body-metric entries (height, weight, BMI), and app settings — is stored locally in the app's private storage on your own device.

03On-device & offline by design

Life Touch does all of its work — logging, timing, statistics, pattern analysis, BMI calculation — directly on your device's processor. None of it is computed in the cloud, because there is no cloud component to compute it in.

You can use every core feature of the app with your phone in airplane mode. An internet connection is never required to open the app, log a habit, view your timeline, read your statistics, or generate the AI prompt text described below.

04No medical advice, diagnosis, or interpretation

Life Touch is a logging and pattern-visibility tool, not a medical device or a diagnostic service. It does not practice medicine, and nothing in the app should be treated as medical advice.

When the app shows you a "pattern," a frequency, an average gap, or a BMI number, it is doing simple arithmetic on data you typed in yourself — nothing more. It does not:

  • Diagnose any medical condition
  • Tell you whether a pattern is healthy, normal, or concerning
  • Recommend treatment, medication, or lifestyle changes
  • Analyze your data using any medical or clinical model

You are always the one reading the raw numbers and deciding what, if anything, to do with them — including whether to bring them to a doctor.

05The "clinical AI prompt" feature

Life Touch can assemble a plain-text summary of your own logged data — for example, frequencies and timing for the habits you choose — worded as a starting prompt that combines an Eastern and Western framing. This feature is a formatting convenience only. It does not analyze, interpret, or diagnose anything.

Here is exactly what happens when you use it:

  • The app reads data that is already on your device and arranges it into readable text.
  • That text is shown to you, on your screen, for you to review before doing anything with it.
  • You choose whether to copy it, and where — if anywhere — to paste it: a doctor's intake form, a message to a specialist, a third-party AI assistant, or nowhere at all.

Generating this text does not send your data anywhere by itself. If you choose to paste it into another app or service, that transfer happens because you did it, on your own device, using your own copy of your own data — and it is then subject to that other service's own privacy terms, not this one.

06Exporting your data

Life Touch lets you export your logged data as a spreadsheet file (.csv). Like everything else in the app, this file is generated locally on your device. Kabastro never receives a copy of it.

Once the file is created, what happens to it is up to you: your device's own share sheet lets you save it, email it to yourself, upload it to your own cloud storage, or send it to anyone you choose. Life Touch's part ends at putting a correct file in your hands.

07Device permissions

Life Touch requests only the device permissions it needs to run its on-device features — for example, storage access so it can save a file when you export your data, and notification access if you turn on optional reminders. None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit your data; they exist purely so the app can read and write to your own device.

08Deleting your data

Because your data only ever exists on your device, you are always in complete control of deleting it. Clearing the app's storage (or uninstalling the app) through your phone's settings permanently removes all of your Life Touch data. There is no server-side copy to ask us to delete, and no account to close, because neither exists.

09Children's privacy

Life Touch is not directed at children and is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, of any age — it collects nothing at all, on our end, by design. If you are a parent or guardian with concerns about a child's use of the app, you can review or remove all locally stored data directly from the device at any time, as described above.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for instance, if a future version of the app introduces an optional feature that changes how data is handled — we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and describe the change here before it takes effect.
